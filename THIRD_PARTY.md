## Code

### IAU SOFA — Standards of Fundamental Astronomy

- **Where**: `sofa/` (249 Fortran sources, release 2023-10-11, compiled into `libsofa.a`);
  and the routine `JD2date` in `src/module_time.f90`, a derived work adapted from
  SOFA's `iau_JD2CAL`.
- **Terms**: SOFA Software License, Copyright (C) 2023 IAU SOFA Board. The six terms and
  conditions are reproduced in full at the end of every file in `sofa/`. In summary:
  use and redistribution are permitted free of charge, provided that derived work is
  marked as such, is not endorsed by SOFA, does not use the `iau`/`sofa` name prefixes,
  and reproduces the conditions intact.
- **Compliance here**: `sofa/` ships unmodified with its notices. The derived routine
  `JD2date` is renamed (no `iau`/`sofa` prefix) and marked in place as SOFA-derived
  and not endorsed by SOFA.
- **Acknowledgement**: in published work using SOFA, acknowledgement is requested —
  see <https://www.iausofa.org>.

### JPL planetary ephemeris reader (`src/PLEPH.for`)

- **Where**: `src/PLEPH.for`, compiled into `libatlas.a`.
- **Terms**: created by the California Institute of Technology (Caltech) under a U.S.
  Government contract with NASA; provided "AS-IS" without warranty; the recipient bears
  all risk and indemnifies Caltech and NASA against third-party claims. The full
  disclaimer is preserved in the file header. No redistribution restriction is stated.

### NRLMSISE-00 atmospheric model (`src/nrlmsise00_*.for`)

- **Where**: `src/nrlmsise00_sub.for`, `src/nrlmsise00_driver.for`; the model is not
  currently reachable from the Atlas code paths (`module_atmosphere` selects its
  tabular density grids instead).
- **Source**: J. M. Picone, A. E. Hedin, D. P. Drob, A. C. Aikin, "NRLMSISE-00 empirical
  model of the atmosphere: Statistical comparisons and scientific issues",
  *J. Geophys. Res.* 107(A12), 1468, 2002.

### LU decomposition routines (`src/module_matrix.f90`)

- **Where**: `LUDCMP` / `LUBKSB` at the end of `src/module_matrix.f90`.
- **Source**: F90 translation by J-P Moreau, Paris, of the LU decomposition algorithms
  from W. H. Press, B. P. Flannery, S. A. Teukolsky, W. T. Vetterling,
  *Numerical Recipes*, Cambridge University Press, 1986.
- These routines are kept with their original attribution and are **not** relicensed by
  this project; their own source terms apply.

### Orbital-elements routine (`src/module_lambert.f90`)

- **Where**: the `Elements` documentation block and `LambertSolution` in
  `src/module_lambert.f90`.
- **Source**: O. Montenbruck and E. Gill, *Satellite Orbits — Models, Methods and
  Applications*, Springer, 2000 (and the accompanying code suite). Kept with
  attribution; not relicensed by this project.

## Data

All data files live under `data/`. They are numerical products of the institutions named
below and are redistributed here so that the toolkit runs out of the box.

| File | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `AtlasData/JPLEPH_DE440` | JPL DE440 planetary and lunar ephemeris, 1949-12-14 → 2150-01-22 (a 200-year subset of the full 1550–2650 file) | Jet Propulsion Laboratory / NASA — <https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/eph_export.html> |
| `AtlasData/EGM2008.txt` | Earth gravity coefficients, degree and order 100 | EGM2008, National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA) |
| `AtlasData/GGM03C.txt` | Earth gravity coefficients, degree and order 360 | GGM03C, Center for Space Research, University of Texas at Austin |
| `AtlasData/grail.txt` | Lunar gravity coefficients, degree and order 80 | GRAIL mission gravity field, NASA / JPL |
| `AtlasData/EOP.txt` | Earth orientation parameters: polar motion, UT1−UTC, LOD, celestial pole offsets | IERS EOP 14 C04 (International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service) |
| `AtlasData/leap.txt` | Leap-second table (TAI−UTC) | IERS Bulletin C — <https://hpiers.obspm.fr/iers/bul/bulc/Leap_Second.dat> |
| `AtlasData/Nutation.dat` | 20-term IAU 1980 nutation series coefficients | IAU 1980 Theory of Nutation |
| `AtlasData/nrlmsise00_cheng/` | Atmospheric density and scale-height grids (Ap, F10.7) | H. W. Cheng, National Astronomical Observatories, CAS; generated by J. S. Tang, Nanjing University |
| `input/isr1062*.sp3` | GPS precise orbit products (SP3-c) | International GNSS Service (IGS) |
| `atlas.png` | Project banner image | Atlas Orbitalis project |

### Imagery
