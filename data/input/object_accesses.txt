notes:
1, obervation type:
	11 - (alpha, delta), celestial equatorial coordinate system
	12 - (A, h, r, rd), horizontal coordinate system, ranging and ranging rate
	13 - (r), ranging
	14 - (x, y, z, xd, yd, zd), GNSS
	15 - (x, y, z), Position
	16 - (A, h), horizontal coordinate system
	17 - (r, rd)

2, from type / to type: 
	type of the transmitter / reciever
	101 - is station
	102 - is satellite

3, from NO. / to NO.: 
	number of the transmitter / reciever
